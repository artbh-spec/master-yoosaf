Biography

Full Biography

Master Mohamed Yoosaf M.V is a dedicated Taekwondo Master, Senior Instructor, Personal Trainer and Physical Education Teacher with 15 years of experience in Taekwondo. Holding a 4th Dan Black Belt, he is a National Kyorugi Referee, National Best Referee Award Holder, National Taekwondo Player and Kerala State Gold Medalist. Forty students have advanced to black belt under his coaching.

Read more Show less

Specializing in children's Taekwondo development, he provides comprehensive coaching in both Poomsae and Kyorugi, focusing on technical excellence, competition readiness, physical fitness and personal development.

Through Excellent Taekwondo Academy Bahrain, his mission is to develop skilled athletes and confident individuals by promoting discipline, respect, dedication and leadership.
